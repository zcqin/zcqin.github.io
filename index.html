01 / About

Profile & research

I study machine learning methods for understanding and connecting information across data, modalities, and real-world tasks.

My scholarly work includes visual-language learning, image captioning, multimodal representation, neural networks, and data-driven prediction. Across these topics, I am interested in how models can make useful connections between visual, textual, and structured information.

This page brings together selected work and links associated with my academic profile. For the most complete and current publication record, please consult my Google Scholar search.

02 / Research

Research interests

Themes reflected in my publications and research projects.

A

Multimodal & visual-language learning

Learning representations that connect images, language, and semantic relationships for tasks such as image-text matching and visual understanding.

B

Computer vision & image captioning

Combining visual features, attention, and language generation to produce informative descriptions and improve visual interpretation.

C

Machine learning & neural networks

Studying learning behavior, model generalization, and network design, including the effects of sparsity and pruning.

D

Data-driven prediction

Applying machine learning to practical prediction problems, including work that combines textual sentiment with financial time-series data.
