01 / ABOUT

A career across ideas,
disciplines, and borders.

My research connects machine learning, visual understanding, language, and reasoning under uncertainty. I study both the foundations of intelligent systems and their applications to complex data.

My academic journey has taken me from Heilongjiang University and the University of Bristol to research appointments at UC Berkeley, Oxford, and Carnegie Mellon. I joined Beihang University in 2009 before moving to VinUniversity.
