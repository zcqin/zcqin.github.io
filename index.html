About

Research focus and academic background

I am a researcher in computational intelligence and intelligent systems, with a focus on machine learning, optimization, and data-driven problem solving. My work aims to connect theoretical understanding with practical, scalable solutions in complex and high-dimensional settings.

My background is shaped by academic collaborations and research environments tied to VinUniversity and the ICMLL Lab at Beihang University, where I have explored intelligent algorithms and scientific applications that bridge learning theory, optimization, and real-world system design.
